## The pledge

As the author of this case study package, I affirm:

1. **Every quotation is traced.** Each quoted passage appears in a dated source in the project's Source Registry, attributed to the correct speaker. No quote was included on the strength of AI recall or secondary aggregation alone.

2. **Every number is attributed.** Financial figures, dates, and metrics cite the source document they came from, and cross-document consistency checks pass.

3. **Verification debt is zero (or disclosed).** All claims the AI generated from general knowledge were logged to `verification-debt.yaml` and subsequently verified, corrected, or removed. Any remaining open items are listed below.

4. **Sources met the minimum gate.** The package rests on at least one primary source in the protagonist's own voice, at least one primary financial source, and independent coverage from multiple publications.

5. **Bias was assessed.** The source mix was analyzed for perspective balance; known gaps (e.g., missing employee, customer, or critic voices) are acknowledged in the Teaching Note.

6. **AI involvement is disclosed.** The package carries the AI methodology disclaimer describing how AI tools were used and how outputs were verified.

7. **A human made the judgment calls.** AI drafted and cross-checked; I decided what matters, what is true, and what ships.
