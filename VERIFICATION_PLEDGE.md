## The pledge

*This is a statement about **work I did and judgments I made** — not a certificate that
the package is error-free. Automated checks assist that work; they do not replace it,
and they miss things. What follows is what I personally stand behind.*

**0. I am responsible for this package.** I directed the AI tools that helped produce
it, I reviewed their output, and I decided what ships. Where something is wrong, that is
mine — "the AI generated it" is not an explanation I would offer and not one I would
accept.

1. **I checked the quotations against their sources.** Each quoted passage was traced to
a dated source in the Source Registry and attributed to the speaker I believe said it. I
did not rely on AI recall or on secondary aggregation. Where a source is an edited or
machine-generated transcript, the package says so and uses the appropriate convention.

2. **I checked the numbers.** Financial figures, dates, and metrics cite the document
they came from, and I reviewed the cross-document consistency results rather than only
noting that the check ran.

3. **Verification debt is zero or disclosed below.** Claims the AI produced from general
knowledge were logged, then verified, corrected, or removed. Anything still open is
listed, with why.

4. **The source base met the minimum gate, and I formed my own view of it.** At least one
primary source in the protagonist's voice, at least one primary financial source, and
independent coverage from more than one publication. I also judged whether that floor is
*adequate for this particular case*, which is a separate question the gate does not
answer.

5. **I assessed bias and named what is missing.** The source mix was analyzed for
perspective balance, and known gaps — missing employee, customer, regulator, or critic
voices — are acknowledged in the teaching materials rather than left for a reader to
discover.

6. **AI involvement is disclosed.** The package carries the methodology note describing
which tools were used and how their output was checked.

7. **A human made every judgment call.** AI drafted and cross-checked. I decided what
matters, what is true, what is fair, and what ships. Where my experience disagreed with
the tool, I followed my experience.

8. **Corrections were re-checked, not assumed.** Where verification findings were fixed,
the fixes were confirmed against the sources rather than taken on the word of whoever
made them, and the package was re-read afterwards for problems the corrections themselves
introduced. I know that "all fixed" is a claim and not a result.

**What this pledge does not claim:** that every sentence has been independently
confirmed, that the automated checks caught everything they could have, or that no error
remains. It claims that a named person did the work of looking, and is accountable for
the result.
