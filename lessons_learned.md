# Lessons Learned

*Your record of what building this case taught you — about the subject, about the
method, and about working with AI. Skills will suggest entries at the moments that
usually produce them: when a check finds something you had accepted, when a source
turns out not to say what you thought, when the tool was confidently wrong.*

*Write the specific thing, not the general lesson. "The 30,000 figure was the
interviewer's, not the executive's, and I carried it as fact in three documents" will
still mean something in a year. "Check attributions carefully" will not.*

*The kit's maintainers keep their own version of this file in
`maintainer/lessons_learned.md`. It is long, and most of the entries are mistakes.
That is what makes it useful.*
